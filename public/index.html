ABOUT APEXTYRE

Your tyre partner,
from selection to fitment.

apexTyre.com is Maharashtra’s multi-brand automotive tyre marketplace, bringing business buyers and individual drivers together with trusted tyre choices. From a single replacement tyre to recurring fleet requirements, our B2B and B2C marketplace makes it easier to explore products, compare fitment details and connect with a local tyre partner.

Our catalogue spans more than 12 leading Indian and global brands, including MRF, Apollo, CEAT, Bridgestone, Goodyear, Michelin, JK Tyre, TVS Eurogrip, Ralco, Maxxis, Continental and Yokohama. We work with manufacturer and distribution partners to make genuine products easier to find, with clear product information and support to verify the exact size, variant, price and availability before an order is approved.

Choosing tyres is about more than a brand name. Our team helps customers consider their vehicle, driving conditions and budget, then connects them with fitting support through our Maharashtra service network. Our customer support is available around the clock to help with product questions, order updates and the next step toward a confident fitment.
